# Isometric Requirements Gap-Check

**Run date:** 2026-07-24 · **Scope:** Biochar Protocol v1.1 (pinned) + 5 referenced modules · **Method:** registry-atom extraction → candidate matching → adversarial verification

**Executive summary.** 328 requirement atoms were checked across 6 modules; 266 candidate gaps were raised and 151 survived adversarial verification (115 false positives dropped): **9 P0, 78 P1, 64 advisory**. The nine P0 findings collapse to six distinct themes, all already tracked in our own P0 checklist but still open: pyrolysis-gas-loss calibration/leak-test structure (P0-04), feedstock EC1–15 eligibility evaluation + >25% issuance cap (P0-01), counterfactual validity lifecycle (P0-02), spillage/lost-mass deduction ledger (P0-05), the embodied-emissions inventory and per-factor records (P0-15), and the BFA three-section eligibility combination rule. Headline **interpretation drifts**: (1) `changes.md`/`versions.json` wrongly deem WBC pollutant thresholds "not applicable" to the pinned ag-soils v1.1 — they are required measurements; (2) reporting-period `started_on` = earliest production start, not earliest feedstock sourcing (§8.6.2, self-acknowledged, deferred); (3) the BFA market-leakage interpretation derives from v1.3 significance tests while we pin v1.2's EC1–EC12 criterion combination; (4) the electricity `ec1_grid_average…ec5_direct_connection` enum misreads EC1–EC5 as alternative sourcing categories when the authority makes them conjunctive qualification criteria; (5) `durability-aggregation.ts` still cites the superseded "Biochar Storage in Soil Environments v1.2" module. Version drift is intentional (protocol re-pinned to v1.1 to match the Certify project) but leaves us two minors behind the latest certified protocol. **Self-test result: 3 of 4 user-flagged known gaps were NOT independently detected (see §Known-gap validation oracle) — treat direct-emissions coverage as a blind spot of this run.**
