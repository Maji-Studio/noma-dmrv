# Cula funding, equity, and valuation research

**Research date:** 2026-08-13  
**Company investigated:** Cula Technologies GmbH, Potsdam, Germany  
**Scope:** Public evidence of fundraising, equity ownership or share sales, grants, and valuation. Currency is EUR unless stated otherwise.

## Bottom line

Cula appears to have raised a **€4 million seed round in August 2024**, but the amount is not disclosed on Cula's own site or in a conventional funding announcement. It is stated by **Malpaux, an early investor and embedded brand/design operator**, on its Cula case study in search-indexed text and on its homepage. The currently rendered case-study body confirms only that the seed round closed in August 2024. This makes €4 million a credible investor/operator claim, but not independently company- or press-confirmed.

Separate primary evidence confirms that Cula received a **€50,000 investment offer** from HPI in February 2023. HPI Ventures now lists Cula as an active seed-stage portfolio company, while HPI's current generic cheque ranges are €100,000–€150,000 at pre-seed and €250,000–€500,000 at seed; those ranges do **not** prove how much HPI ultimately invested in Cula.

The clearest equity evidence is the August 2024 German register event: stated share capital rose from **€25,000 to €35,579**. A register-data aggregator shows Heartcore Capital Fund V K/S holding **5,972 nominal shares (16.79%)**, the four founders' holding companies holding **6,250 each (17.57% each)**, and nine smaller shareholders hidden. If the €4 million was entirely new primary equity issued in that capital increase, the mechanically implied valuation is approximately **€9.45 million pre-money / €13.45 million post-money**. That inference is useful but not a disclosed transaction valuation and is sensitive to options, convertibles, secondary sales, nominal-price timing, and whether all €4 million belonged to this capital increase.

No reliable public evidence of an acquisition, company sale, crowdfunding campaign, or disclosed secondary share sale was found. No defensible public valuation was found beyond the conditional register-based inference above. A lead-generation site’s **$5 million** “valuation” is an automated revenue-multiple estimate and conflicts with better funding evidence; it should not be treated as a transaction valuation.

[HPI Ventures Cula portfolio page](https://www.hpiventures.com/portfolio/cula-technologies) (primary investor source).

HPI Ventures' current fund page says its standard cheque sizes are **€100,000–€150,000 for pre-seed** and **€250,000–€500,000 for seed**. [HPI Ventures SEED](https://www.hpiventures.com/funds/hpi-ventures-seed) (primary investor source). These are present-day generic ranges, not historical Cula deal terms, so they must not be added to the €50,000 or treated as evidence of Cula's total.

**Limit:** Neither amount paid nor valuation is public. The shareholding pages identify the owners' holding companies, not a direct Malpaux shareholder line, so this should not be over-interpreted as proof of the investment structure.

## Conditional valuation and dilution analysis

These calculations are **inferences, not disclosed deal terms**.

### Mechanical dilution from the registered capital increase

Assuming the four founder holding companies owned the entire original €25,000 and issued no transfers:

- New nominal shares: `€35,579 − €25,000 = €10,579`
- New-share percentage post-increase: `€10,579 / €35,579 = 29.7338%`
- Original-holder percentage post-increase: `€25,000 / €35,579 = 70.2662%`
- Mechanical dilution of the original 100% position: `100% − 70.2662% = 29.7338 percentage points`

This is consistent with the four founder holdings shown at 17.57% each. It does not establish which new shares were bought for cash, issued for services, converted from instruments, or transferred secondarily.

### Implied valuation if €4 million bought all 29.7338% new shares

Under the strong assumptions that (1) Malpaux's €4 million figure is accurate, (2) the whole €4 million was primary cash into Cula, (3) all new shares in the August capital increase priced pari passu in that round, and (4) no options, convertibles, debt, or secondary consideration affect the denominator:

- Post-money valuation: `€4,000,000 / 29.7338% = approximately €13.45 million`
- Pre-money valuation: `€13.45 million − €4 million = approximately €9.45 million`
- Implied price per €1 nominal new share: `€4,000,000 / 10,579 = approximately €378.11`

### Heartcore cheque implied by the same pro-rata assumption

Heartcore holds **5,972 / 35,579 = 16.7852%** post-increase, or **5,972 / 10,579 = 56.45%** of the new nominal shares. If every new share received the same price and Heartcore bought all of its shares in the €4 million round, its implied investment would be:

- `€4,000,000 × 5,972 / 10,579 = approximately €2.26 million`

This is **not a confirmed Heartcore cheque**. Preferential terms, prior convertibles, a mixture of primary and secondary transactions, nominal shares issued at different prices, or another transaction structure could materially change it.

For an intentionally broad scenario check, if the €4 million bought only Heartcore's 16.7852% post-round stake, the implied values would be approximately **€23.83 million post-money** and **€19.83 million pre-money**. Taken together with the all-new-shares case, the mechanically implied scenario range is therefore roughly **€9.45 million–€19.83 million pre-money / €13.45 million–€23.83 million post-money**. This is not a confidence interval: the actual transaction can fall outside it because earlier investments may have converted, other holders may have bought shares in the seed, and economic rights need not track nominal shares exactly.

Its amount appears to capture only HPI's 2023 offer and misses Malpaux's €4 million seed claim and the register capital increase. [CB Insights Cula profile](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/cula-technologies) (secondary commercial database; incomplete/internally inconsistent).

- Prospeo states **“No funding,”** estimates annual revenue at **$1,539,990**, and derives an estimated **$5 million valuation** from an industry multiple. It discloses no company accounts, deal, ownership percentage, or transaction supporting these figures. [Prospeo Cula profile](https://prospeo.io/c/cula-technologies-revenue) (low-confidence automated estimate). This is not evidence of what investors paid or what Cula was valued at.

## What can safely be said

- **Confirmed offer:** €50,000 HPI investment offer in February 2023.
- **Confirmed investor relationships:** HPI Ventures, Malpaux, and Heartcore; the register-derived shareholder view additionally shows nine smaller holders but hides their identities.
- **Credible but not independently confirmed amount:** €4 million seed round, closed August 2024, stated by early investor/operator Malpaux.
- **Confirmed equity event:** registered capital rose by €10,579 to €35,579 in August 2024; post-increase new-share fraction was 29.73%.
- **Conditional valuation inference:** about €9.45 million pre-money / €13.45 million post-money if the €4 million was wholly primary cash for all new shares at one price.
- **Confirmed grant/support:** EFRE/ILB “Gründung innovativ” support, amount undisclosed.
- **Not found:** verified acquisition/sale price, crowdfunding, disclosed secondary-sale proceeds, or a company-announced valuation.

## Search limits

The official German register portal is session-driven and its original shareholder document was not accessible in this research session. Registry mirrors were therefore used for the capital and shareholder details, cross-checked across OpenRegister, CompanyHouse, Viaductus, Fusionbase, and handelsregister.ai. The official filing should be obtained directly from the German register portal before relying on the cap table for legal, investment, or transaction decisions. Private-company financing may also use undisclosed side letters, liquidation preferences, options, convertibles, or secondary components that cannot be reconstructed from nominal GmbH share capital alone.
