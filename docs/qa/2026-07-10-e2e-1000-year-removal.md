## Defects fixed during the run

1. The 1000-year credit-batch preview ignored sample replicate R0 and
   non-reactive-carbon statistics and therefore reported the batch as not ready.
2. The removal snapshot incorrectly required a facility soil-temperature value
   for 1000-year durability. Soil temperature is a 200-year-only input.
3. The Isometric 1000-year inertinite property mapping was wrong. Sandbox
   validation confirmed the accepted pair is `dimensionless_ratio` plus
   `inertinite_fraction`, with a dimensionless 0–1 value.
4. An Isometric-created GHG Statement whose removals were already linked could
   not be adopted locally because the empty-statement guard only examined open
   removals. The create flow now reconciles one exact remote draft and still
   fails closed on multiple matches.
