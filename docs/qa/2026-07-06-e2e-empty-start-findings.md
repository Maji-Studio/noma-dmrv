## What works well (verified, worth preserving)

- **Quick-Add + seedEntityCache**: feedstock-type and storage-bin quick-adds create AND auto-select in the parent dropdown; feedstock-type quick-add auto-fixes Usage to the parent workflow (Pyrolysis) and filters categories accordingly; storage-bin quick-add pre-sets Storage Type + locks Feedstock Type from context.
- **Live derivations & stock-awareness**: wet+moisture → live "Dry: X kg"; source-bin dropdown shows "… · 4,250 kg remaining"; order product dropdown shows "500 kg in bin"; production-run form shows a live Process-Flow with computed yield %.
- **Transport auto-fill**: supplier stored distance auto-prefills feedstock transport distance (8.3 km, CERT); customer stored distance auto-prefills delivery distance (45 km); ORS road-distance CALC works.
- **Certification transparency**: post-create toasts name what's still missing ("Still needed to certify: Telemetry readings"); credit-batch detail has a Certification checklist ("2 OF 4 CLEARED, 1 issue open") listing exact gaps; batch form explains ADR-0020 emission attribution inline.
- **Correct gating (fail-closed)**: registry-link gate hides cert operational routes until linked; New Removal wizard disables CONTINUE and shows "0 of 1 batches ready" with per-issue reasons; application requires a Delivered delivery; sample form is durability-aware and enforces R₀ for the 1000-year tier + surfaces the ≥3-replicate rule; formulation ratio-sum blocks >100% with a live "Total ratio: 150%".
- **#253 appears FIXED**: deep-linking a batch under the wrong active facility reconciled the URL + selector back to the batch's facility and rendered it (no mismatch/leak).
- **Detail pages** use dry mass correctly (credit-batch "PRODUCT MASS 0.47 t dry") — only the Sankey (F14) is off-basis.
